This manual explains, in plain English, how to use AI tools at work without getting yourself (or your employer) into hot water. It is written like a friend would explain it: step by step, no jargon walls, no condescension.

Who this is for: anyone who uses — or is curious about using — AI tools like chat assistants, coding helpers, or meeting summarizers at work.

What this is not: legal advice. Every workplace and jurisdiction is different. If you need legal answers, talk to a qualified professional.

The one-sentence version

Treat AI like a very helpful, very chatty intern who sometimes overshars: give it no secrets, check its work, and be honest about when you use it.
